I am a third-year Ph.D. student at the Harris School of Public Policy, University of Chicago. My research spans political economy, development, and economic history. Before my Ph.D., I earned a joint bachelor's degree in Economics from HSE University and the New Economic School (summa cum laude) and worked as an intern researcher at the HSE Laboratory of Sports Studies.

My current work includes a study of Soviet-era anti-religious repression and its long-run effects on religiosity in Russia. Besides research and teaching at the Harris School, I lead the organization of the NES High School Economics Competition, part of my broader involvement with economics olympiads for high school students in Russia.

You can contact me at dstarikov@uchicago.edu.
